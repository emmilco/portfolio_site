Grask — Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 15, 2026

Summary

Grask stores all your data on your device. Nothing is collected, transmitted, or shared. There are no accounts, servers, analytics, or third parties.

What we collect

Nothing. Grask does not collect any personal information, usage data, device identifiers, location, or analytics of any kind.

What is stored on your device

Grask stores the tasks and notes you create in a database on your device. This data:

  • Never leaves your device.
  • Is included in your device's standard iCloud or iTunes backups if you have those enabled, and is governed by Apple's privacy terms while there.
  • Is deleted permanently when you uninstall Grask, unless it has been restored from an iOS backup.

Third parties

None. Grask does not include any third-party SDKs, analytics libraries, advertising networks, crash reporters, or tracking tools.

Children's privacy

Grask does not collect data from any user, including children. It is safe for all ages under this policy.

Changes to this policy

If this policy is updated, the effective date above will change. Because Grask does not collect user data, changes will typically reflect clarifications rather than shifts in practice.
