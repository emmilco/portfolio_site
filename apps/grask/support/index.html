Grask — Support

Grask is a visual task organizer for iOS.

Getting help

For questions, bug reports, or feature requests, email dev@emmilco.com.

Reporting a bug

When reporting a bug, please include:

  • Your iOS version
  • Your device model (for example, iPhone 15)
  • What you were doing when the bug occurred
  • What you expected to happen
  • What actually happened

Response time

Grask is maintained by an individual developer. Bug reports are addressed on a best-effort basis, typically within a few days.
